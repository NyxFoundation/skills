# Formalization Landscape: Economics, Quantum Info, and Others (July 2026)

## Economics
- **EconCSLib**: Core library for Computational Economics, Game Theory, Mechanism Design (arXiv:2606.16144).
- **vNM Utility Theorem**: Full formalization of the expected utility theorem (arXiv:2506.07066).
- **Bounded Rationality**: formalization of Simon's satisficing using FFSD (arXiv:2507.07052).
- **Stochastic Dominance**: Geometric formalization of 1st-order stochastic dominance in N-dim (arXiv:2505.12840).
- **DeFi/AMM**: Formalization of Constant-Product AMMs (arXiv:2402.06064).
- **Social Choice**: Voting theory impossibility theorems via SAT+Lean (arXiv:2208.06907).

## Quantum Information Theory (QIT)
- **Lean-QIT**: Broad infrastructure for finite-dim QIT; covers Schumacher, Holevo-Schumacher-Westmoreland, and entanglement-assisted capacity (arXiv:2607.09632).
- **Lean-Quantum**: Focused on analytical foundations, Rényi relative entropy DPI, and generalized Quantum Stein's Lemma (arXiv:2607.05492).
- **TNLean**: Tensor Network Theory (MPS basic theorem) formalization via multi-agent LLM workflow (arXiv:2607.07857).
- **QNN**: Formalization of Quantum Neural Network expressivity and trainability (arXiv:2607.12981).
- **Quantum Optimization**: Machine-verified proof of the FGG conjecture for QAOA (arXiv:2606.29687).
- **Quantum Stein's Lemma**: Deep formalization showing original proof gaps (arXiv:2510.08672).

## Chemical Physics & Hardware
- **Chemical Physics**: Langmuir/BET adsorption, Boyle's Law, Newtonian mechanics (arXiv:2210.12150).
- **Ripple**: Chemical Reaction Networks (CRNs) and computable reals, Turing-completeness (arXiv:2607.13531).
- **ASIC/Thermodynamics**: Thermodynamic reservoir computing and Bitcoin mining ASIC communication (arXiv:2601.12032).

## Key Trends
- **AI-Driven**: Shift toward "AI-Formalized" workflows where LLM agents produce the bulk of the Lean code.
- **Gap Discovery**: Formalization is consistently identifying gaps/errors in "hand-written" proofs in physics and QIT.
- **Consensus Need**: Multiple overlapping libraries (especially in QIT) highlight the need for a centralized "mathlib-like" standard for each domain.
