# Event Risk Matrix

Common high-impact risks for professional summits and their mitigation strategies.

| Risk Event | Probability | Impact | Mitigation / Response Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sponsor Withdrawal** | Low | Critical | Maintain a "Waitlist" of secondary sponsors. Diversify tiers so no single sponsor provides >30% of budget. |
| **Key Speaker Cancellation** | Medium | High | Pre-identify "Backup Speakers" for each session. Ensure a recording or virtual option is ready. |
| **Venue Infrastructure Failure (WiFi/Power)** | Medium | Critical | On-site technical rehearsal 1 week prior. Dedicated LTE/5G backup routers. Power strip audit. |
| **Low Attendee Turnout (<50% Goal)** | Medium | High | Implement "Early Bird" deadline to gauge interest. Scale up aggressive SNS promo/paid ads 4 weeks out. |
| **Budget Overrun** | Low | Medium | Use a "Budget DB" with strict Approval/Actual tracking. Allocate a 10-15% contingency fund. |
| **Staffing Shortage (No-shows)** | Low | Medium | Over-hire volunteers by 20%. Create a clear role-based manual so any staff can cover basic tasks. |
